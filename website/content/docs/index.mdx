Lumiface has three parts, plus your backend:

- **Server** (`server/`): FastAPI. Takes a WebSocket stream of frames per session, clocks it itself and reads every blink, smile, turn and flash reflection from its own 68-point landmarks; InsightFace ArcFace for matching, MiniFASNet and the CVPR-2024 ResNet50 gate for passive anti-spoof. SQLite by default, Postgres through `DATABASE_URL`. Multi-tenant: every project has an API key and its own policy.
- **Flutter package** (`packages/lumiface`): `FaceVerifyView` plus the headless `FaceVerifyController`. ML Kit on iOS and Android, MediaPipe on the web.
- **React SDK** (`packages/lumiface-react`): `LumifaceView`, `useLumiface` and the same headless controller, MediaPipe in the browser.
- **Your backend** holds the project key: it creates sessions (`POST /v1/sessions`) and reads verdicts (`GET /v1/sessions/{id}`). `examples/backend` is a complete one in ~100 lines; the device SDKs cannot take a key.
