title: Lumiface documentation
description: Self-hosted face verification with active liveness, a policy per project, and camera SDKs for Flutter and React.
---

Lumiface has three parts, plus your backend:

- **Server** (`server/`): FastAPI. Takes a WebSocket stream of frames per session, clocks it itself and reads the face's move into the oval and the flash reflection from its own detector; InsightFace ArcFace for matching against the photo the session was created with, MiniFASNet and the CVPR-2024 ResNet50 gate for passive anti-spoof. SQLite by default, Postgres through `DATABASE_URL`. Multi-tenant: every project has an API key and its own policy. It keeps no face between sessions.
- **Flutter package** (`packages/lumiface`): `FaceVerifyView` plus the headless `FaceVerifyController`. TensorFlow Lite BlazeFace on Android, Apple Vision on iOS, TensorFlow.js BlazeFace on the web.
- **React SDK** (`packages/lumiface-react`): `LumifaceView`, `useLumiface` and the same headless controller, TensorFlow.js BlazeFace in the browser.
- **Your backend** holds the project key and the users' photos: it creates sessions with the photo (`POST /v1/sessions`) and reads verdicts (`GET /v1/sessions/{id}`). `examples/backend` (Python) and `examples/backend-node` (TypeScript) are complete ones in ~100 lines; the device SDKs cannot take a key.

Three people usually share the work. Pick your door:

| You are | Read | You touch |
|---|---|---|
| **Front-end** (the app) | [Flutter](/docs/flutter) or [React](/docs/react), then [Reason codes](/docs/reason-codes) for the copy | one view, a `sessionProvider` that calls your backend, an `onResult` that tells it you are done |
| **Back-end** (the key and the users) | [Your backend](/docs/backend), then [API](/docs/api) | two REST calls, `session_id → user` on your side, the decision |
| **Installing the server** | [Get started](/docs/get-started), then [Server](/docs/server) and [Security](/docs/security) | `docker compose` or `uv run uvicorn`, the environment, one project per app, TLS in front |

Then [Concepts](/docs/concepts) for the vocabulary the three of you share, and the [Policy reference](/docs/policy-reference) when a threshold needs tuning.
